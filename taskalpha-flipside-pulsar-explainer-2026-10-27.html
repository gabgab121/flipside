How money and data move

Who pays whom

Clients pay Flipside. Flipside buys accepted data from TaskAlpha under one monthly fee, and TaskAlpha pays the people who collected it. Flipside charges TaskAlpha for staff time spent on TaskAlpha work.

MoneyDataNothing runs from Flipside to a TaskAlpha contributor or vendor.

Who does what

Flipside staff wear two hats

Which hat you are wearing decides which company carries the cost.

Customer hat · Flipside cost
  • Client work and project design
  • Tier 2 acceptance
  • Field sourcing, logistics and workplace partners
  • Legacy contributors
Support hat · recharged to TaskAlpha monthly
  • Contributor and vendor marketing under the TaskAlpha brand
  • support@taskalpha.io help desk, onboarding and KYC
  • Vendor admin and the Payout team
  • First-line platform QA
  • Platform engineering, with the IP sitting with TaskAlpha

Rate cards, policies and agreements are TaskAlpha’s. Flipside staff apply them and do not set or negotiate them; anything outside the rate card goes to the Singapore director. Eric Suzuki takes that role from January 2027. Nobody logs hours: the recharge follows an FTE allocation by role, confirmed quarterly by the Service Delivery Manager.

Ground rules

What we don’t do

  1. Pay, top up or front money from Flipside for a TaskAlpha contributor or vendor.
  2. Agree rates outside the TaskAlpha rate card.
  3. Run contributor or vendor marketing under the Flipside name.
  4. Put legacy contributors in a TaskAlpha-paid project before they are onboarded.
  5. Sign Pulsar partner organisations on Flipside paper.
  6. Give TaskAlpha work beyond data collection.
